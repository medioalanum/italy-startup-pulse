Methodology

How to read this data

What is measured

Aggregated counts of startups in the official Italian startup registry, grouped by region and primary economic sector.

Dimensions

Region and Sector are independent cuts of the registry. Their totals must not be combined into a cross-tab.

Source and cadence

MIMIT quarterly reports publish aggregate tables. Net change is the difference between consecutive totals, not a new-entry count.

Source: MIMIT quarterly reports.
